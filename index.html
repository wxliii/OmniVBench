01 / DATASET OVERVIEW

Omni-R2V Dataset

Comprehensive Coverage of R2V Tasks

The first large-scale public R2V training dataset covering heterogeneous reference types across seven task families, spanning single-reference tasks and multi-reference compositions.

Industry-Grade Training Data

340K processed training samples built primarily from professional video footage, spanning live-action, 2D animation, and 3D animation.

Reusable R2V Construction Pipelines

Task-specific pipelines construct reference–target pairs and corresponding training instructions, providing a reusable recipe for omni-R2V data construction.
