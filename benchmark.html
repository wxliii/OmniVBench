OmniVBench

OmniVBench evaluates how faithfully video generation models preserve and compose the intended reference factors. It spans 813 cases across seven task families, combining case-specific checklists for reference fidelity and instruction realization with video quality evaluation.

RF / REFERENCE FIDELITY

Preserve the intended factors

Assess whether task-relevant information from the references is faithfully retained.

IR / INSTRUCTION REALIZATION

Use them as instructed

Assess factor selection, disentanglement, binding and compliance with the requested target.

VQ / VIDEO QUALITY

Evaluate the resulting video

Assess visual quality separately from reference preservation and instruction execution.
